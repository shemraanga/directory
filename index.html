01 / The mission

A launchpad for young creators.

Born from the wider vision of the Smile Urban Youth Initiative, SHEMRAANGA brings technology closer to young people and their communities. We are building an energetic home for practical digital skills, creative problem-solving, collaboration and youth-led innovation.
