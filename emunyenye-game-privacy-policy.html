Privacy Policy

Your play stays yours.

Emunyenye is an offline reflex game developed by SHEMRAANGA. It is designed to be enjoyed without an account, internet connection, personal data collection, or permission requests.

Works offlineNo sign-up No data collectionNo permission requests Ages 13+
Privacy at a glance: Emunyenye does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or use your personal information. Gameplay is designed to remain on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Emunyenye does not collect personal information or personal data from users. You do not need to create an account, sign in, provide your name, email address, phone number, location, or other identifying information to play.

2. Offline Gameplay

Emunyenye is designed to work offline. The core game does not require an internet connection or communicate with SHEMRAANGA servers during gameplay.

3. Local Game Data

The app may store limited game information locally on your device, such as your best score and app preferences, so that these features remain available between play sessions. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to SHEMRAANGA.

You can generally remove locally stored game information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to the controls provided by your device operating system.

4. Device Permissions

Emunyenye does not request access to sensitive device features or personal information such as your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, photos, or files for its gameplay.

5. Accounts, Advertising and Tracking

Emunyenye does not require registration or login. The app does not use personal data for advertising, profiling, cross-app tracking, or behavioral targeting, and SHEMRAANGA does not sell or share personal information collected through the game because the game does not collect such information.

6. Children's and Teen Privacy

Emunyenye is intended for individuals aged 13 and above. Because the app does not collect personal information, SHEMRAANGA does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Emunyenye. If you believe a privacy issue involving a minor has occurred, please contact us using the details below.

7. Data Security

Emunyenye minimizes privacy risk by avoiding the collection and transmission of personal data. Any limited game state stored by the app is kept locally on the user's device and is subject to the device's security controls.

8. Third-Party Services

The game itself is designed without third-party services that collect personal data for gameplay, analytics, advertising, or authentication. Distribution platforms such as Google Play may independently process information under their own terms and privacy policies; that processing is controlled by those platforms rather than by Emunyenye.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

SHEMRAANGA may update this Privacy Policy if Emunyenye's features or legal requirements change. Any revised policy will identify a new effective date. If a future version of the app introduces data practices that differ materially from those described here, this policy and applicable store disclosures should be updated accordingly.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Because Emunyenye does not collect personal information, there is no personal-data account or profile within the game for you to access, correct, or delete. Questions or privacy requests can still be directed to SHEMRAANGA using the contact information below.
